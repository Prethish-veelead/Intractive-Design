# Brand voice

How `[BRAND]` sounds in Thai and English. Use it for every headline, product text, button, email and LINE message.

## Who we sound like

A friend who works as a makeup artist: warm, honest, a little playful, never pushy.
She knows her craft, explains simply, and wants you to feel good as you are.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Warm and polite | Cold, corporate or stiff |
| Confident and clear | Loud, salesy, "BUY NOW!!!" |
| Honest about what a product does | Exaggerated or medical ("cures acne", "whitening in 3 days") |
| Inclusive of every Thai skin tone | Focused on one ideal of beauty |

## Thai (default language)

- Polite feminine ending **ค่ะ** in customer messages, help pages and LINE replies. Headlines and buttons have no ending.
- Use **คุณ** for the customer. Never กู/มึง, and avoid overly cute slang (e.g. ตัวแม่, ปังมาก) in product text. It's fine in social posts.
- Keep common beauty loanwords in Thai script: ลิปสติก, คุชชั่น, บลัชออน, อายไลเนอร์, มาสคาร่า.
- Product and shade names stay in English (Velvet Kiss, Mulberry). Descriptions are Thai.
- **Never** use "ขาว", "ผิวขาวใส" or "whitening" as a promise. Describe shades with skin tone names (ผิวขาว, ผิวสองสี, ผิวแทน) only to match shades.
- Short sentences. Thai has no spaces between words, so give long text room (line height is set by the design system).

## English

- British spelling to match the Thai market convention (colour, favourite).
- Sentence case for headings and buttons ("Add to bag", not "Add To Bag").
- Plain words: "lasts 12 hours" beats "long-lasting performance technology".

## Words we use / avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| ติดทนนาน, long-wear | กันน้ำ 100%, permanent |
| ผิวสาวไทย, every Thai skin tone | ผิวขาว as a goal, whitening, fairness |
| ผ่านการทดสอบในอากาศร้อนชื้น, tested in hot, humid weather | guaranteed, miracle |
| เฉดสี, shade | สีผิวดี / สีผิวไม่ดี |
| ส่วนผสม, ingredients | chemical-free (everything is a chemical) |

## Patterns

- **Product tagline** (one line): what it is + the main benefit. "ลิปสติกเนื้อแมตต์นุ่มละมุน ไม่ทำให้ปากแห้ง"
- **Button**: verb first, 1–3 words. "เพิ่มลงตะกร้า", "ค้นหาเฉดสี", "Add to bag"
- **Error/help**: say what happened and what to do next, politely. "ขออภัยค่ะ ไม่พบหน้านี้ ลองกลับไปที่หน้าแรกนะคะ"
